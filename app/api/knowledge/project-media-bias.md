# Project Case Study: ANA — Spotting the Bias in News

## Overview
- **Product Title**: ANA: Spotting the Bias in News
- **Role**: UX Researcher & Designer
- **Timeline**: 12 weeks
- **Team**: 2 UX Designers, 2 UX Researchers
- **Tags**: AI, UX Research, Media Bias, Explainable AI

## The Problem
Young people (18-28) are increasingly trapped in "information bubbles," consuming news from sources that reinforce their pre-existing political views. Traditional news aggregators present articles without clarifying underlying perspective or editorial bias.

Our primary research showed:
- **66% of users** could not distinguish article topic/substance from editorial bias.
- **82% of users** had never used an AI-powered news aggregator before.
- Users feel confused and distrustful because they cannot determine if a story leans left, center, or right.

## The Solution
We designed and researched ANA, an AI-powered news aggregator that identifies and explains media bias in real-time. Instead of just filtering news, ANA focuses on transparent, explainable AI explanations.

### Key Features
1. **Explainable AI Bias Indicators**: Highlighting biased language directly within articles using inline color overlays to explain the semantic difference between neutral and biased reporting.
2. **Multi-Perspective Aggregator**: Grouping articles about the same topic from different ideological spectrums side-by-side to help users break out of information bubbles.
3. **Transparent Bias Analysis**: Displaying the AI model's confidence scores and reasoning for classifying an article as having a specific political leaning.
