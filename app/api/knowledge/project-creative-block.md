# Project Case Study: Navigating Creative Block

## Overview
- **Product Title**: Navigating Creative Block
- **Role**: UX Researcher
- **Timeline**: 4 months
- **Tags**: UX Research, Feature Discovery, Creativity, Behavioral Analysis

## The Problem
Creative block is a pervasive issue for artists and designers, yet technology largely ignores it. Existing productivity or design tools (like Pinterest or Headspace) focus on organization or general stress relief, rather than targeting the mental mechanisms of "feeling stuck" (loss of confidence, choice paralysis, burnout).

## The Research
We conducted a comprehensive, mixed-methods user research study to understand the mental patterns of creative block and uncover design opportunities for a digital assistant.

### Research Methodology
- **16 Semi-Structured Interviews**: In-depth qualitative discussions with professional illustrators, designers, writers, and software developers.
- **Behavioral Analysis**: Mapping the cognitive stages of creative block (Ideation Friction, Mid-Project Burnout, Post-Completion Anxiety).
- **Concept Testing**: Presenting low-fidelity feature cards to participants to measure resonance.

### Key Results
- **10 Core Features Discovered**: Discovered concrete digital interventions (e.g. low-stakes playgrounds, structured prompts, visual progress markers, accountability systems).
- **Behavioral Framework**: Synthesized qualitative data into a behavioral framework outlining how tools can help creatives transition from passive scroll loops back to active creation.
